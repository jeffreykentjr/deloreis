# A few notes for a Holmes world

- *A Study in Scarlet*, part I, chapter 1: the narrator is John H. Watson, a
  doctor who served at Maiwand. Murray helped him reach the British lines.
  Watson says he was hit in the shoulder by a Jezail bullet. Stamford introduces
  the possibility of sharing rooms with Sherlock Holmes.
- *The Sign of the Four*, chapter 1: Watson describes a Jezail wound in his leg.
  Keep the two wound descriptions open; the passages do not establish that they
  refer to the same injury.
- *The Man with the Twisted Lip*: Watson's wife offers to send “James” to bed.
  If we decide to interpret James as his first name and allow only one first
  name, check that interpretation against the name John.
- *The Final Problem*: Watson concludes that Holmes died at Reichenbach.
  *The Empty House*: Holmes returns and describes his escape. Keep Watson's
  earlier conclusion inspectable when accepting the later account.

The [source index](sources.md) links to the texts and records the decision IDs.
The scripted example includes further details from those stories; it does not
infer them automatically from this short note.
