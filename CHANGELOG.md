# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-05

- A Sherlock Holmes casebook follows sourced claims through continuity checks,
  disputed accounts, a retcon, and an alternative branch. The book includes a
  browsable wiki and guides for modelling a world from existing notes.
- The first release is available on crates.io and as Linux x86-64 and macOS ARM archives.
- Contract 2.0.0 is the sole served contract. Retired versions and operations
  are refused with a successor.
- The default build includes `.dli` text tools, project initialization and
  import. MCP advertises a compact fiction profile by default and an extended
  profile with `--tools all`.
- Workspaces refresh after commits; naming findings respect the configured
  dialect; query rows can identify supporting claims. Conflicting proposed
  values on functional slots receive a nonblocking warning.
- Contested accounts and retcons retain their history across export and import.

## Previous contract history

The 1.x series introduced structured fact-object authoring, standing and
retcons, functional and inertial predicates, temporal queries, decisions,
predicate and entity reads, and durable conditional attacks. The 0.x series
provided earlier rendered authoring and wire shapes. These wire versions are
retired; the decision ledger records their detailed history.
